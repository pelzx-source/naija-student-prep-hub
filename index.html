Study Smarter

Simple habits that can make your study sessions more effective.

01. Set clear goals

Decide exactly what you want to complete before starting a study session.

02. Avoid distractions

Keep unnecessary notifications and social media away while studying.

03. Practise questions

Don't only read. Solve questions to check whether you actually understand.

04. Use active recall

Close your textbook and try to explain what you have learned from memory.

05. Review regularly

Review older topics so that important information stays fresh in your memory.

06. Take healthy breaks

Short breaks can help you stay focused during longer study sessions.

Exam Preparation

WAEC, NECO & JAMB

Build a consistent preparation routine instead of waiting until the last minute.

WAEC

Prepare for your West African Senior School Certificate Examination.

  • Understand your subjects
  • Practise past questions
  • Improve time management
  • Review difficult topics

NECO

Build strong subject knowledge and practise examination questions.

  • Follow your syllabus
  • Practise regularly
  • Review corrections
  • Study consistently

JAMB

Prepare strategically for the UTME with regular practice.

  • Know your subject combination
  • Practise CBT-style questions
  • Work on speed and accuracy
  • Track your performance
